# Products

Products are the goods and services you sell to customers and buy from vendors. Every invoice line and bill line points to a product, so you create products once and reuse them in both flows.

> **In simple words:** This is your catalogue of things you sell and buy.

## In this group

- [Manage products](./manage-products.md): create products, set prices and taxes, add attributes and variants, and print labels.
