# Invoices

The **Invoices** module handles the money side of your business. Use it to bill customers, record bills from vendors, track payments, and issue credit notes or refunds when something changes after billing.

Everything in the module follows one simple flow: a **partner** (customer or vendor) receives or sends a **document** (invoice or bill), the document is **posted**, and then a **payment** settles it.

> **In simple words:** Invoices is where you create the paper trail for money you are owed and money you owe.

## How the module is organised

### Customers

Manage what you sell and who you sell to.

- [Manage customers](./customers/manage-customers.md): create customers and keep their contacts, addresses and bank accounts.
- [Create an invoice](./customers/create-invoice.md): bill a customer and confirm it.
- [Create a credit note](./customers/create-credit-note.md): correct or cancel part of a posted invoice.
- [Register a payment](./customers/register-payment.md): record money received from a customer.

### Vendors

Manage what you buy and who you buy from.

- [Manage vendors](./vendors/manage-vendors.md): create vendors and keep their details.
- [Create a bill](./vendors/create-bill.md): record an invoice received from a vendor.
- [Create a refund](./vendors/create-refund.md): record money a vendor returns to you.
- [Register a payment](./vendors/register-payment.md): record money paid to a vendor.

### Products

- [Manage products](./products/manage-products.md): create the goods and services you invoice, with prices, taxes and variants.
