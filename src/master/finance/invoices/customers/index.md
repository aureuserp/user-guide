# Customers

The customer side of **Invoices** follows one flow. You add a customer, bill them with an invoice, and record their payment. If you need to cancel or reduce a bill, you issue a credit note. Each step links to the one before it, so your records stay consistent.

- [Manage customers](./manage-customers.md): create customer records with contacts, addresses and bank accounts.
- [Create an invoice](./create-invoice.md): bill a customer, confirm the invoice and pay it.
- [Create a credit note](./create-credit-note.md): cancel or adjust an invoice, or record a return.
- [Register a payment](./register-payment.md): record money received from or sent to a customer.
