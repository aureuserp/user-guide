# Bank accounts

A bank account record stores the account details of a customer or vendor. You need it to record payments and to reconcile them against invoices and bills.

> **In simple words:** Save the bank details of the people you pay or get paid by.

## Create a bank account

1. Go to `Invoices → Configurations → Bank Accounts`.
2. Click **New Bank Account**. A modal opens.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/bank_create.png" alt="Bank Accounts list with the New Bank Account button" />

3. Fill in the fields.

   - **_Account Number:_** Enter the full bank account number.
   - **_Bank:_** Pick a bank from the list, or click the plus icon to create one.
   - **_Account Holder:_** Select a registered customer or vendor.

4. To create a bank, fill in the new modal.

   - **_Name:_** Enter the bank name.
   - **_Bank Identifier Code:_** Enter the bank code.
   - **_Email:_** Enter the bank email.
   - **_Phone:_** Enter the bank phone number.
   - **_Address:_** Enter the bank address.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/bank_create_1.png" alt="New bank account modal with account number, bank and account holder" />

5. Save the record.

::: info
Payment reconciliation uses these accounts during invoice and bill processing.
:::
