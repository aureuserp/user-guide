# Invoice settings

Invoice settings control how products behave on invoices and bills. Right now the page offers one option, which decides whether lines can use different units of measure.

> **In simple words:** Switch this on if you sell the same product in different units, such as pieces or dozens.

## Manage products

1. Go to `Invoices → Settings → Manage Products`.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/manage_products_navigation.png" alt="Menu path to the Manage Products setting" />

2. Set the toggle.

   - **_Unit of Measure:_** Lets you sell and buy products in different units of measure.
   - When on, you pick the unit (pieces, boxes, dozens) for each product on the document.
   - When off, products use only their default unit.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/manage_products_page.png" alt="Manage Products settings page with the Unit of Measure toggle" />

3. Click **Save Changes**.

::: info
With the option on, you can sell one product as 1 unit or as 1 dozen, depending on what the customer needs.
:::
