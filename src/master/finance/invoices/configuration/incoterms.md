# Incoterms

Incoterms are international trade terms. They state who handles shipping, insurance and tariffs between buyer and seller. They matter most for cross-border sales and purchases.

> **In simple words:** An incoterm is a short code that says who pays for what during shipping.

## Create an incoterm

1. Go to `Invoices → Configurations → Incoterms`.
2. Click **New Incoterms**. A modal opens.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/incoterms_create.png" alt="Incoterms list with the New Incoterms button" />

3. Fill in the fields.

   - **_Code:_** Enter exactly 3 characters, such as CIF or FOB.
   - **_Name:_** Enter the full name or a description.

   <ImagePopup src="/images1/invoices/incoterms_create_1.png" alt="New incoterm modal with code and name" />

4. Save the record.

::: info
You can use incoterms on both customer and vendor transactions.
:::
