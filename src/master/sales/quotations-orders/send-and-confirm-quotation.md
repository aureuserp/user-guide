# Send and confirm a quotation

When the quotation is ready, send it to the customer. After the customer accepts, confirm it. The quotation then becomes a sales order and you can deliver and invoice it.

> **In simple words:** Send the offer, wait for a yes, then click Confirm to turn it into an order.

## The state bar

The bar at the top of the quotation shows where it is:

- **_Quotation:_** The draft you are preparing.
- **_Quotation Sent:_** The quotation was emailed to the customer.
- **_Sales Order:_** The quotation is confirmed.

A quotation can also be **Cancelled**. This status shows in the list.

## Buttons on a quotation

Open a quotation from `Sales → Orders → Quotations`. The page has the tabs **View**, **Edit**, **Invoices** and **Deliveries**.

- **_Send By Email:_** Opens an email window.
- **_Preview:_** Shows the quotation as the customer sees it.
- **_Confirm:_** Turns the quotation into a sales order.
- **_Cancel:_** Cancels the quotation.
- **_Delete:_** Removes the quotation.

### Send a quotation by email

1. Click **Send By Email**. The **Send Quotation By Email** window opens.
2. Check **Partners**. The customer is selected for you. You can add more.
3. Edit the **Subject** and **Description**, if needed.
4. Check the **Attachment**. A PDF of the quotation is added for you.
5. Click **Submit**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/invoice_view_send_email.png" alt="Send By Email window with recipients, subject, message and the PDF attachment" />

::: info
The customer needs an email address. If no recipient has one, Aureus shows a warning and sends nothing.
:::

### Confirm a quotation

1. Click **Confirm**.
2. Aureus opens the order. The state bar now shows **Sales Order**.

The order lines now show the **Delivered** and **Invoiced** quantities. Use the **Invoices** and **Deliveries** tabs to follow the order. See [Manage orders](./manage-orders.md).

### Cancel a quotation

1. Click **Cancel**.
2. In the **Cancel Quotation** window, choose an option:

   - **_Send & Cancel:_** Cancels the quotation and emails the customer. You can edit the **Partner**, **Subject** and **Description** first.
   - **_Cancel:_** Cancels the quotation without an email.
   - **_Close:_** Closes the window and keeps the quotation.

A cancelled quotation shows the **Set as Quotation** button. Click it to reopen the quotation.

::: warning
**Delete** removes the quotation. Use **Cancel** instead if you want to keep a record.
:::
