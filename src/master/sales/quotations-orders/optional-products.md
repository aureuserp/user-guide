# Optional products

The **Optional Products** tab lets you list extra products on a quotation, next to the products you are already offering. Use it for add-ons or alternatives you want to mention to the customer. These lines are kept apart from the **Order Line** tab.

> **In simple words:** Optional products are extra products saved with the quotation, separate from the ordered lines.

## Add an optional product

1. Open a quotation, or click **New Quotation** under `Sales → Orders → Quotations`.
2. Click the **Optional Products** tab.
3. Click **Add Product**.
4. Fill in the line:

   - **_Product:_** Choose the product. Each product can be listed once. Required.
   - **_Quantity:_** The number of units. The default is 1. Required.
   - **_UOM:_** The unit of measure. Required. It shows only when Unit of Measure is turned on.
   - **_Unit Price:_** The price of one unit. It is filled in from the product. Required.
   - **_Discount (%):_** A discount on the line. It shows only when Discount is turned on.

5. Click **Create** or **Save changes**.

<ImagePopup src="/images1/sales/optional_products_tab.png" alt="Create Quotation page with the Optional Products tab and the Add Product button" />

To remove a line, use its delete icon and confirm.

## When the tab is visible

- The tab is available only while the quotation is in **Quotation** or **Quotation Sent**. It is hidden once the order is confirmed or cancelled.
- On the view page, the tab shows only when the quotation has optional products.

::: info
The view page lists the optional products as a read-only table. To sell one of them, add it as a line on the **Order Line** tab. See [Create a quotation](./create-quotation.md).
:::
