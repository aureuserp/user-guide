# Quotations and orders

This group covers the first half of the sales flow: your customers, the offers you send them, and the orders that result. You prepare a quotation, send it, and confirm it when the customer agrees. The confirmed quotation becomes a sales order that you deliver and invoice.

> **In simple words:** A quotation is an offer. When the customer says yes, it becomes an order.

## The flow

1. Add the customer. See [Manage customers](./manage-customers.md).
2. Create a quotation with the products and prices. See [Create a quotation](./create-quotation.md).
3. Send the quotation by email. See [Send and confirm a quotation](./send-and-confirm-quotation.md).
4. Confirm the quotation. It becomes a sales order.
5. Follow the order, deliver it and invoice it. See [Manage orders](./manage-orders.md).
