On this page

Configuration ​

Configuration holds the one-time setup of the Sales module. Settings decide which features are available. Configurations hold the lists those features use, such as categories, packagings and tags.

In simple words: Settings switch features on. Configurations fill them with data.

Settings ​

Open Sales → Settings to manage module-wide options.

  • Settings: products, invoice policy, pricing, and quotation and order options.

Configurations ​

Open Sales → Configurations to manage these lists.

  • Product categories: group products in a tree.
  • Product attributes: define options such as size or colour for variants.
  • Packagings: define pack sizes for a product.
  • Tags: colour labels for sales orders.
  • UOM categories: units of measure and how they convert.

Activity Plans and Activity Types appear in the same menu. They work the same way across Aureus, so they are covered in Activities.
